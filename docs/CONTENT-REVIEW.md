# Public content maintenance

Keep project summaries, case studies, the Writing index, terminal and résumé consistent.
Use `src/data/projects.ts` for catalogue order and `src/data/resume.json` for both résumé
formats. Do not copy private investigation notes or internal repository metadata into this
public documentation.

## Claim review

- Describe functional contributions separately from formal employment titles and dates.
- Keep company work, independent client work and independent products distinct.
- Distinguish release-baseline features, development branches, staged validation and actual
  operational deployment/adoption. A passing build does not establish production rollout.
- For education, identify the institution attended and affiliation separately. Confirm exact
  awarding wording and personal completion dates before adding them. Do not infer a campus.
- Omit unverified metrics, business outcomes, customers, testimonials and qualifications.
- Design completion does not prove printing, distribution or commercial impact.
- Use labeled illustrations when an approved, privacy-safe real capture is unavailable.
  Never publish internal records, identifiers, endpoints, backups or authentication data.

## Editorial and metadata consistency

Preserve established publication dates when verified in the original article source and
use explicit ISO dates for Article structured data. Project timelines are not publication
dates. Keep article/index technology tags aligned. Maintain established URL slugs when
needed for continuity, even when titles are improved.

The current catalogue separates the new factory application from the legacy Fabric Store
implementation. The secondary brand case study records completed designs; its associated
article explains the process rather than duplicating the deliverable list.

Read-only source inspection and private evidence review support these editorial decisions.
Detailed private evidence is intentionally outside this public repository document.
